utterpy 0.0.6 carries utter 0.0.6, which adds TitaNet-small as a speaker model, rates how well the acoustic model hears the audio on every result, and keeps a stream's heap flat however long it runs. Without a speaker model and with the new rating keys taken out, the output is the same as 0.0.5.

## Added

`SpkModel(path)` now also opens a TitaNet-small directory, converted from the published ONNX by utter's scripts/titanet_convert.py; `dim()` gives 192. Hand it to a recognizer as before, with `KaldiRecognizer(..., spk_model=)` or `SetSpkModel`. The recognizer embeds each word's span on a thread of its own, and the recognizer's own thread costs about what it costs with no speaker model. A word's entry carries `spk`, `spk_frames`, `spk_start` and `spk_end`, published on the first result read after 40 ms more audio, so the same result carries it on every machine; its evidence arrives a median 200 ms after the word ends. Partials and finals carry no top-level evidence with TitaNet. The model needs audio at 16 kHz or faster; the vosk-model-spk-0.4 x-vector still opens and stays the choice below that.

On utter's speaker page (205 speakers, 1980 probe words), one word through TitaNet-small clears a gate at 1% false accept 81.2% of the time, against 41.6% for the x-vector. As with the x-vector, build speaker profiles from enrolment audio with the model you will use; vectors from one model do not carry to the other.

`SpkModel.embed(data, sample_rate=16000)` gives the embedding of a span of little-endian float32 samples without a recognizer, with the GIL released. It works with either model layout.

Every partial and final now carries the acoustic model's certainty and the sound outside words, with no setting to turn them on: `certainty_words` and `certainty_outside` (1 minus the normalised entropy of the network's output, counted over `words_frames` and `outside_frames`), and `band_db`, `band_sd_db`, `rise_start_sample`, `rise_ms` and `rise_db` for the frames in no named word. Every word entry carries certainty over its own span. Only differences within one model mean anything. TD-17 in utter has the details.

## Fixed

A stream's heap no longer grows with its length: at 20 minutes without a speaker model, 17.9 MiB where 0.0.5 held 246 MiB.

The measurements are in utter's [release notes](https://github.com/pyscape/utter/blob/main/docs/release-notes/v0.0.6.md).

## Install

utterpy is not on PyPI yet. Take the wheel for your platform from this release's assets:

```bash
pip install utterpy-0.0.6-cp39-abi3-manylinux_2_28_x86_64.whl
```

Wheels for Linux x86_64 and aarch64 (manylinux 2_28 and musllinux 1.2), Windows x64 and arm64, and macOS x86_64 and arm64, Python 3.9 and later through the stable ABI. Every wheel carries a build-provenance attestation, and the release attaches its Sigstore bundle:

```bash
gh attestation verify utterpy-0.0.6-cp39-abi3-manylinux_2_28_x86_64.whl --bundle utterpy-v0.0.6.sigstore.json --owner pyscape
```
