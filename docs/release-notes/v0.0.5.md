utterpy 0.0.5 carries utter 0.0.5, which adds speaker evidence. The binding exposes it the way the vosk wheel does: `SpkModel` and `KaldiRecognizer(..., spk_model=)` or `SetSpkModel`. Without a speaker model, the output is the same as 0.0.4.

## Added

`SpkModel(path)` opens a vosk speaker model directory in the vosk-model-spk-0.4 layout, and `dim()` gives its vector length. Hand it to a recognizer with `KaldiRecognizer(model, rate, grammar, spk_model=spk)` or `rec.SetSpkModel(spk)`. `SetSpkModel(None)` removes it. Audio already fed in the current stream counts toward the evidence. The recognizer refuses a speaker model when its audio rate is below the model's 8 kHz.

While a speaker model is set, a span of at least 25 pooled 10 ms frames carries `spk` (the vector), `spk_frames`, `spk_start` and `spk_end` (samples since the recognizer was built). These keys sit at the top level of every partial and final, and on every word entry in results and alternatives. On the plain final, `spk` and `spk_frames` come before `text`, where vosk puts them. The vectors follow Kaldi's network but not the wheel's normalisation or frame selection, so a speaker enrolled with the vosk wheel is enrolled again; [TD-14](https://github.com/pyscape/utter/blob/main/docs/td/0014-every-word-carries-the-speaker-evidence-of-its-own-span.md) says why. The details are in utter's [release notes](https://github.com/pyscape/utter/blob/main/docs/release-notes/v0.0.5.md).

The release also attaches the provenance envelope (`utterpy-v0.0.5.intoto.jsonl`) beside the Sigstore bundle.

## Install

utterpy is not on PyPI yet. Take the wheel for your platform from this release's assets:

```bash
pip install utterpy-0.0.5-cp39-abi3-manylinux_2_28_x86_64.whl
```

Wheels for Linux x86_64 and aarch64 (manylinux 2_28 and musllinux 1.2), Windows x64 and arm64, and macOS x86_64 and arm64, Python 3.9 and later through the stable ABI. Every wheel carries a build-provenance attestation, and the release attaches its Sigstore bundle:

```bash
gh attestation verify utterpy-0.0.5-cp39-abi3-manylinux_2_28_x86_64.whl --bundle utterpy-v0.0.5.sigstore.json --owner pyscape
```
